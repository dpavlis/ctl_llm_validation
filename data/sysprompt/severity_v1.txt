Review severity (owner ruling): ERROR is only a compile failure, a certain runtime failure, or a violation of a requirement the task states. A risk that depends on the data (a nullable field that may be null, a lookup that may miss, a string that may not parse, a format letter that is valid but probably not what was meant) is WARNING, not ERROR, unless the task says the data makes it certain. Style, naming and optional improvements are INFO. The verdict is FAIL only when there is at least one ERROR.
